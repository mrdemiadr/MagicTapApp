Just tap. It clicks.

MagicTap lets your Magic Mouse click with a tap, no pressing needed. Tap with one finger to left-click, two fingers to right-click, and hold three fingers to drag. It’s a small app that lives quietly in your Mac’s menu bar.

Free to use. One file works on every Mac, Apple Silicon or Intel.

Download for macOS

MagicTap.dmg

  1. Open MagicTap.dmg and drag MagicTap into your Applications folder.
  2. If macOS shows a warning, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  3. Turn on Accessibility permission for MagicTap so your taps can send clicks.
